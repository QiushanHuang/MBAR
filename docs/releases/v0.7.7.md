# MBAR 0.7.7

2026-11-27 · Build 26 · macOS 27 · Apple Silicon

## 中文

右键 MBAR 菜单栏入口，选择 **下拉栏外观预设 → 静透**。下拉栏解锁、接收键盘输入或切换到设置时，都保留原玻璃在设置窗口获得焦点时的透色、磨砂和边缘折射。

- 在 **设置 → 下拉栏 → 外观…** 中可单独调整着色和磨砂，并另存为个人预设。五种材质在图标条、网格、列表和预览中采用相同的圆角。
- 玻璃上的图标和文字可选「随背景」「跟随系统」「固定黑色」或「固定白色」。随背景模式为每个图标或文字分别选择统一的前景色，保留细线、镂空和透明度。彩色原图保留原色。

先退出 MBAR，再将新版拖入「应用程序」替换旧版。发行包采用 ad-hoc 签名，未经 Apple 公证。

## English

Right-click the MBAR menu-bar entry and choose **下拉栏外观预设 → 静透**. The shelf keeps the color transmission, frosting and refractive rim of the original glass as it appears while Settings has focus, including when you unlock the shelf or navigate by keyboard.

- Adjust tint and frosting independently in **设置 → 下拉栏 → 外观…** and save a custom preset. All five materials share the same outer corners in the strip, grid, list and preview.
- Glass icons and labels support background-adaptive, system, fixed black and fixed white foregrounds. Adaptive mode chooses one foreground per icon or label, preserving fine strokes, cutouts and partial transparency. Full-color artwork keeps its original colors.

Quit MBAR and replace the copy in Applications. The downloads are ad-hoc signed and have not been notarized by Apple.
